DB RDL To Power Clean
▼
Movement
Stand feet shoulder width apart, DB in each hand in front of shins. Hinge at hips and complete RDL. Then at the top, drive feet into the floor and thrust hips forward, bringing DBs just above shoulders. Bring DBs back to floor, repeat combo.
▶ Watch Video
Coaching Cues
›"Hinge first — feel the hamstring stretch before you reverse"
›"Power comes from the hips — explode through the floor"
›"DBs stay close to your body — zip the jacket on the way up"
›"Catch soft on the rack — elbows up and around the DBs"
Muscles
Hamstrings Glutes Shoulders Lower Back Traps Core
Modifications
⬇️
EasierLighter DBs. Or split into separate movements — RDL first, then a separate clean.
⬆️
HarderHeavier DBs. Add a press at the top of each clean.
🩹
Low Back IssuesLight DBs. Reduce range — only pull to mid-shin. Focus on the hinge pattern.
🤸
Push Up to Down Dog
▼
Movement
Complete a push up, then keep the hands and feet in position and shift the weight back to a downward dog. Elevate the hands if needed.
▶ Watch Video
Coaching Cues
›"Push up first, then sit hips back and up"
›"Long spine in the down dog — press the floor away"
›"Full push up depth every rep"
›"Reset to plank before the next push up"
Muscles
Chest Hamstrings Shoulders Calves
Modifications
⬇️
EasierElevate the hands on the bench.
⬆️
HarderSlow the push up and hold the down dog longer.
🩹
Wrist IssuesUse push-up handles or fists.
🤸
Leg Raise on Bench
▼
Movement
Lying flat on the bench, hands under the glutes if needed, start with both legs straight up in the air. Lower them together with control until just before they tap the floor, then raise back to the top and repeat.
▶ Watch Video
Coaching Cues
›"Press your low back into the bench the whole time"
›"Control the lower — that's where the work is"
›"Keep the legs long — only a soft bend in the knees"
›"Stop the lower before your back arches off the bench"
Muscles
Lower Abs Core Hip Flexors Quads
Modifications
⬇️
EasierBend the knees to 90° to shorten the lever.
⬆️
HarderPause 2 seconds with the legs just off the floor each rep.
🩹
Lower Back IssuesReduce the range and never let the back arch.
🔵 Rack
🔔
KB Lateral Lunge Alt.
▼
Movement
Stand feet hip-width with a KB at your chest. Step one leg out to the side and sit back into that hip and heel, keeping the other leg straight. Press through the foot to return to standing, alternating sides.
▶ Watch Video
Coaching Cues
›"Sit back into the hip — push the glute behind you"
›"Working knee tracks over the toes, other leg stays long"
›"Chest tall — don't fold over the bell"
›"Drive through the whole foot to stand"
Muscles
Glutes Quads Adductors Core
Modifications
⬇️
EasierReduce depth or hold the KB at the chest with no added load.
⬆️
HarderPause at the bottom or hold the bell lower in a goblet position.
🩹
Knee IssuesLimit depth and keep the knee tracking behind the toes.
🔁
TRX Alligator Pull
▼
Movement
Face the anchor, hands in front, arms straight. The right arm goes overhead while the left arm pulls down. Return, flip the hands, and repeat.
▶ Watch Video
Coaching Cues
›"One arm up, one arm down — opposite directions"
›"Straight arms — pull from the shoulders"
›"Flip cleanly between reps"
›"Body stays square to the anchor"
Muscles
Upper Back Shoulders Lats Core
Modifications
⬇️
EasierWalk the feet back to reduce the angle.
⬆️
HarderWalk the feet forward for more resistance.
🩹
Shoulder IssuesShorten the range near the top.
🤸
Res. Band Lateral Shuffle
▼
Movement
With the band around the legs, shuffle laterally in one direction, staying low, then switch and repeat on the other side.
▶ Watch Video
Coaching Cues
›"Stay low the entire shuffle"
›"Push the band out with every step"
›"Quick feet, controlled base"
›"Don't let the knees cave in"
Muscles
Glutes Adductors Quads Core
Modifications
⬇️
EasierShorten the shuffle range or stand taller.
⬆️
HarderWiden the steps or lower the stance.
🩹
Knee IssuesSlow the pace and keep steps small.
