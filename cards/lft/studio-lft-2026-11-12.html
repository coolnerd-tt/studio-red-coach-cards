Brawn Cycle · Advanced Strength Block 2 · Week 6 of 6
Building on the strength cycle using more advanced protocols to continue to drive strength gains.
Limiting the range of motion and adding a pause on the floor will build strength around the sticking point of the bench press which is typically right off the chest. Press Focus (form and proper loading) with slightly higher accessory volume and intensity than the last 3 weeks.
