Brawn Cycle · Advanced Strength Block 2 · Week 6 of 6
Building on the strength cycle using more advanced protocols to continue to drive strength gains.
Bilateral to Unilateral Loading of the Main LFT will challenge the eccentric (lowering) portion of the lift to increase strength adaptations. Hinge Focus (form and proper loading) with slightly higher accessory volume and intensity than the last 3 weeks.

Focus on finding today’s range of motion and activating the supporting muscle groups for the big LFT

Alt. BW Reverse Lunge:45 per side
Step straight back and drop the back knee toward the floor, torso tall. Drive through the whole front foot to stand, and alternate sides.

Fire Hydrant:45 per side
Keep the core engaged and hips still while laterally raising one leg using the glutes. This bracing will apply to the Main LFT in the workout.
  • Lift with the glute, not the low back
  • Hips stay square to the floor

  • Reach long as the hips go back
