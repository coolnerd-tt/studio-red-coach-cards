An athletic phase that includes explosive and powerlifting-based protocols to increase rate of force production.
The Main LFT should feel like a grind or max effort, focus on moving fast with control. Full body explosive work.

Band Squat and SA Row:45 per side
Squat and then row 1 arm to stand up. This bracing will apply to the Main LFT in the workout.

  • One arm at a time, ribs down

Main LFT15:00
Create tension before every lift
2a

Set catches to lowest position. Explosively pull barbell into lower ribs, slightly using the legs to create momentum.
  • Pull explosively into the lower ribs
  • Bar resets on the catches every rep

DB Pullover3-4 x 12
Lying on bench. Hold DB with both hands. Engage core, lower weight behind head for a big stretch. Pull back to top of movement using lats.

  • Bell in the hand opposite the front leg
