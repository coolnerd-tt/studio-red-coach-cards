An athletic phase that includes explosive and powerlifting-based protocols to increase rate of force production.
The Main LFT should feel like a grind or max effort, focus on moving fast with control. Press focus, keep load at 6/10 and intensity at a 10/10

1a
Band Over And Back1:30
Take the band overhead with lateral tension, then straight down the spine rather than away from the back behind.
  • Keep tension wide the whole way
  • Go only as wide as the shoulders allow
1b
Band Face Pull and Press1:30
Band set up for each partner. Pull into face then press over head. Lower, repeat. This bracing will apply to the Main LFT in the workout.
  • Pull to face height, elbows high
  • Press overhead without leaning back
1c
Floor Slides1:00
Lying on back. Get forearms as close to ground as possible.
  • Forearms stay as close to the floor as you can
  • Low back stays pressed down
1d
Paused Push Up1:00
Pause at bottom position then explode back to plank.
  • One second pause at the bottom
  • Elbows back at 45, not flared out
1e
MB Hinged Chest Slam1:00
Hinged over in wide stance. Explosively press ball repeatedly into the ground for 3 quick reps, rest briefly, go again.
  • Wide stance, stay hinged over
  • Three quick slams, then reset
Main LFT15:00

2a
Landmine SA Push Press3-5 x 5e
Take 1-3 warm up sets to build to a working weight that you can quickly move for 5 reps. Dip with legs and use upward momentum to press out.
  • Dip with the legs, then press
  • Ribs down — no leaning back to finish

Stand tall with knees slightly bent and abs tight. Bring the Kb outside of the ear behind the head and around the other side. Repeat opposite direction
  • Tight circle — keep the bell close to the head
