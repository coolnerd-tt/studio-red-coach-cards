Squeeze the shoulder blades together at the top of each movement. Eyes follow the elbow.
  • Let the band pull you under, then squeeze back
  • Rotation comes from the upper back

Main LFT15:00
Create tension before every lift
2a
Chin Up (assisted as needed)6, 6, 4, 2
First set is warmup set. Build to HEAVY 2. Experienced members can go for 2 sets of 2 if there is time.
  • Palms facing you, elbows pull to the ribs
  • Chin over the bar, then lower all the way
2b
Band Pull Apart4 x 15ALLEY
Hold band in both hands, pull apart until band touches chest. Repeat. Keeping tension on the band.
  • Let it touch the bottom of the sternum
  • Keep tension the entire rep
