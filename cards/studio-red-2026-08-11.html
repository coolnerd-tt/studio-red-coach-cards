Squat to Split Jump
▼
Movement
Complete a squat, then a split jump lunge on each side and back to start. Continue to repeat.
▶ Watch Video
Coaching Cues
›"Squat first — full depth, full standup before the split jump"
›"Switch FULLY in the air — not on the ground"
›"Land soft into the lunge — back knee almost grazes the floor"
›"Reset to squat between split jumps — that's one rep"
Muscles
Quads Glutes Hamstrings Calves Core
Modifications
⬇️
EasierStep into the lunges instead of jumping. Reduce squat depth.
⬆️
HarderAdd height to the squat jump. Hold DBs at sides. Faster tempo.
🩹
Knee IssuesStep switch instead of jumping. Reduce squat depth.
💪
DB Power Clean
▼
Movement
Stand feet shoulder-width, DB in each hand in front of the shins. Drive the feet into the floor and thrust the hips forward, bringing the DBs just above the shoulders. Bring the DBs back to the floor and repeat.
▶ Watch Video
Coaching Cues
›"Hips snap them up — the arms just guide"
›"Catch soft at the shoulders"
›"Flat back every time you reach down"
›"Stand tall to finish each rep"
Muscles
Glutes Shoulders Quads Core
Modifications
⬇️
EasierLighter DBs or reduce the range.
⬆️
HarderHeavier DBs, quicker cycle.
🩹
Lower Back IssuesElevate the DBs on a riser to shorten the pull.
🤸
Sit Up on Bench
▼
Movement
Lie flat on the bench, hands to either side of the head, complete a sit up. Lie back down with control and repeat.
▶ Watch Video
Coaching Cues
›"Lead with the chest, not the neck"
›"Full sit up — reach tall at the top"
›"Slow lower — don't just flop back"
›"Exhale as you rise"
Muscles
Core Hip Flexors
Modifications
⬇️
EasierCross the arms over the chest instead of by the head.
⬆️
HarderHold a light weight at the chest.
🩹
Lower Back IssuesShorten the range and go slow.
🔵 Rack
🏐
Ball Ground to Shoulder alt.
▼
Movement
Stand with MB in front of you. Squat to grab ball, back flat. Stand and bring ball to shoulder. Roll it over. Repeat other side.
▶ Watch Video
Coaching Cues
›"Squat to pick up — back flat, chest tall, lift with the legs"
›"Drive through the hips — let the hip explosion send the ball up"
›"Roll the ball over the shoulder — don't muscle it with the arms"
›"Reset stance every rep — feet under hips before the next pickup"
Muscles
Glutes Quads Shoulders Hamstrings Core Lower Back
Modifications
⬇️
EasierLighter ball. Bring to chest height instead of shoulder. Reduce squat depth.
⬆️
HarderHeavier ball. Add a press overhead at the top of each rep.
🩹
Low Back IssuesLighter ball. Reduce squat depth and ensure flat back on every pickup.
🔔
KB Tactical Lunge
▼
Movement
Holding the KB at your side, complete a reverse lunge with the opposite foot and pass the KB underneath the front leg at the bottom. Step forward and repeat on the opposite side.
▶ Watch Video
Coaching Cues
›"Pass the bell under the front thigh at the bottom"
›"Stay tall — don't dive down to make the pass"
›"Back knee hovers, doesn't slam"
›"Smooth handoff — the bell never rests"
Muscles
Quads Glutes Core Obliques
Modifications
⬇️
EasierLunge without the pass, holding the bell at the chest.
⬆️
HarderSlow the descent and pause for the pass.
🩹
Knee IssuesLimit depth and shorten the step.
🤸
Res. Band Shuffle Row
▼
Movement
Hold the resistance band in each hand, arms by your sides, standing on the middle of the band. Hinge at the hips and take one step to the right, row the hands to the hips and back down, then step left and repeat, alternating sides.
▶ Watch Video
Coaching Cues
›"Hinge first, then row"
›"Step and reset — quick and controlled"
›"Elbows drive back on the row"
›"Flat back the whole set"
Muscles
Upper Back Glutes Lats Core
Modifications
⬇️
EasierSkip the shuffle — row in place.
⬆️
HarderBigger steps or stand on more band.
🩹
Lower Back IssuesShorten the hinge and lighten the band tension.
