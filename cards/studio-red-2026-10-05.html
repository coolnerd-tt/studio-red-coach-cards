🏋️ Bench
💥
Box Jump
▼
Movement
Feet hip-width apart. Squat back like you're sitting in a chair, then explode up to jump onto the bench. Step down and repeat.
▶ Watch Video
Coaching Cues
›"Load the hips, swing the arms — explode up"
›"Land soft in a quarter squat — quiet feet"
›"Full foot on the box every time"
›"Step down, never jump down"
Muscles
Quads Glutes Calves Hamstrings
Modifications
⬇️
EasierStep up instead of jumping, or use a lower target.
⬆️
HarderAdd height or pause briefly between reps to reset power.
🩹
Knee IssuesReplace with step ups — no jumping.
💪
DB Curl to Press
▼
Movement
Stand tall with palms up. Curl the DBs to the shoulders, rotate the hands as you press overhead, then return to the shoulders and curl back down to the hips.
▶ Watch Video
Coaching Cues
›"Curl, rotate, press — in that order"
›"Ribs down as you press overhead"
›"Elbows in on the curl"
›"Control it all the way back down"
Muscles
Biceps Shoulders Triceps Core
Modifications
⬇️
EasierLighter DBs, or split the curl and press.
⬆️
HarderPause at lockout on every rep.
🩹
Shoulder IssuesPress only to eye level.
🤸
Hollow Rock on Bench
▼
Movement
Press the low back down, lift the shoulder blades and legs into a C shape, then rock like a rocking chair with the arms extended overhead and legs out straight.
▶ Watch Video
Coaching Cues
›"Make a banana — low back glued down"
›"Rock from the whole body, not the neck"
›"Arms stay by the ears"
›"Stay in the C shape the whole time"
Muscles
Core Lower Abs Hip Flexors Shoulders
Modifications
⬇️
EasierHold the hollow position without rocking, knees bent.
⬆️
HarderLonger lever — arms and legs fully extended.
🩹
Lower Back IssuesBend the knees and shorten the hold.
🔵 Rack
🔔
KB Swing w/ Lateral Step
▼
Movement
KB Swing while taking a lateral step, alternating sides from right to left.
▶ Watch Video
Coaching Cues
›"Step out as the KB swings UP — timing makes the move flow"
›"Land balanced before the next hinge — don't rush the step"
›"Hips power the swing — not the arms"
›"Stay athletic — soft knees, chest tall through the float"
Muscles
Glutes Hamstrings Core Shoulders Adductors Grip
Modifications
⬇️
EasierLighter KB. Skip the step — stay planted, just swing.
⬆️
HarderHeavier KB. Larger lateral step for more travel. Quicker tempo.
🩹
Low Back IssuesLighter KB. Smaller range — chest-height swings only.
🔁
TRX Hinge to Overhead
▼
Movement
Facing the anchor, hold a TRX handle in each hand with tension on the straps. Hinge at the hips with a flat back, then drive the hips forward to stand tall, finishing with the arms reaching overhead. Lower back to the hinge with control.
▶ Watch Video
Coaching Cues
›"Hinge from the hips — push them back, soft knees"
›"Flat back the whole way down"
›"Snap the hips through and reach tall overhead"
›"Let the straps support you — stay in control, don't yank"
Muscles
Glutes Hamstrings Shoulders Core
Modifications
⬇️
EasierReduce range — hinge to a comfortable depth and reach to shoulder height.
⬆️
HarderSlow the tempo and pause at full overhead reach.
🩹
Lower Back IssuesKeep the hinge shallow and the back flat throughout.
🔁
TRX Mountain Climbers
▼
Movement
Feet in the straps in a high plank. Drive one knee toward the chest keeping the other leg extended, then switch legs quickly, alternating throughout.
▶ Watch Video
Coaching Cues
›"Hips stay low — don't pike up"
›"Shoulders stacked over the wrists"
›"Drive the knee all the way in"
›"Quick switches, controlled hips"
Muscles
Core Hip Flexors Shoulders Quads
Modifications
⬇️
EasierSlow the pace, or do them on the floor.
⬆️
HarderSpeed up while keeping the hips level.
🩹
Wrist IssuesPerform from the forearms or on the floor.
