🏋️ Bench
💪
DB Curtsy Lunges Alternating
▼
Movement
DBs at the sides. Step one leg backward and across behind the other, dropping the back knee toward the ground with the torso upright, then return and switch legs.
▶ Watch Video
Coaching Cues
›"Cross behind, like a curtsy"
›"Torso stays upright — don't twist"
›"Front knee tracks over the toes"
›"Drive through the front heel to stand"

Box Jump
▼
Movement
Feet hip-width apart. Squat back like you're sitting in a chair, then explode up to jump onto the bench. Step down and repeat.
▶ Watch Video
Coaching Cues
›"Load the hips, swing the arms — explode up"
›"Land soft in a quarter squat — quiet feet"
›"Full foot on the box every time"
›"Step down, never jump down"
Muscles
Quads Glutes Calves Hamstrings
Modifications
⬇️
EasierStep up instead of jumping, or use a lower target.
⬆️
HarderAdd height or pause briefly between reps to reset power.
🩹
Knee IssuesReplace with step ups — no jumping.
🤸
Bear Hold Shoulder Taps
▼
Movement
From all fours, tuck the toes and lift the knees an inch off the floor so they sit just under the hips. Holding that position, lift one hand and tap the opposite shoulder, then switch.
▶ Watch Video
Coaching Cues
›"Knees hover — just an inch off the floor"
›"Back flat, like a tabletop"
›"Don't let the hips rock as you tap"
›"Squeeze everything and breathe"
Muscles
Core Shoulders Quads Obliques
Modifications
⬇️
EasierKeep the knees on the floor while you tap.
⬆️
HarderSlow each tap down and hold it at the shoulder.
🩹
Wrist IssuesHold the position on fists or forearms without the tap.
🔵 Rack
🔁
TRX Y Pull
▼
Movement
Hold the TRX handles with the arms extended, leaning back with the body straight. Pull the arms overhead into a Y position, then return under control.
▶ Watch Video
Coaching Cues
›"Make a Y with the arms overhead"
›"Body stays in one straight line"
›"Squeeze the shoulder blades down and back"
›"Slow, controlled return"
Muscles
Upper Back Rear Delts Traps Core
Modifications
⬇️
EasierWalk the feet back to stand more upright.
⬆️
HarderWalk the feet forward and pause in the Y.
🩹
Shoulder IssuesReduce the overhead range — stop at eye level.
🏐
Ball Clean to Press
▼
Movement
Feet shoulder-width with the med ball in the middle. Sit the hips back, grab the ball, then drive the feet into the floor and thrust the hips forward, bringing the ball to the chest. Press it to the ceiling, return it to the floor, and repeat.
▶ Watch Video
Coaching Cues
›"Hips do the clean — the arms just guide it"
›"Stand all the way up before you press"
›"Press straight overhead — finish tall"
›"Flat back every time you reach for the floor"
Muscles
Glutes Shoulders Quads Core
Modifications
⬇️
EasierClean to the chest only — skip the press.
⬆️
HarderHeavier ball or add a squat before the press.
🩹
Shoulder IssuesPress to eye level instead of full overhead.
🤸
Push Up to Toe Touch
▼
Movement
Complete a push up, then push the hips up into an inverted V and tap the right hand to the left toe. Return to the push up position and repeat, alternating the tapping hand.
▶ Watch Video
Coaching Cues
›"Full push up before the reach"
›"Hips high in the V position"
›"Reach across to the opposite toe"
›"Keep the legs as straight as you can"
Muscles
Chest Core Shoulders Hamstrings
Modifications
⬇️
EasierPush up from the knees and tap to the shin.
⬆️
HarderPause in the V and reach further through the toe.
🩹
Wrist IssuesUse push-up handles, or elevate the hands on the bench.
