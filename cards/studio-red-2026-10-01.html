Squat to Split Jump
6 EACH SIDE
▼
Movement
Complete a squat, then a split jump lunge on each side and back to start. Continue to repeat.
▶ Watch Video
Coaching Cues
›"Squat first — full depth, full standup before the split jump"
›"Switch FULLY in the air — not on the ground"
›"Land soft into the lunge — back knee almost grazes the floor"
›"Reset to squat between split jumps — that's one rep"
Muscles
Quads Glutes Hamstrings Calves Core
Modifications
⬇️
EasierStep into the lunges instead of jumping. Reduce squat depth.
⬆️
HarderAdd height to the squat jump. Hold DBs at sides. Faster tempo.
🩹
Knee IssuesStep switch instead of jumping. Reduce squat depth.

Push Up to Toe Tap
6 REPS
▼
Movement
Complete a push up, then push the hips up into an inverted V and tap the right hand to the left toe. Back to the push up, then repeat tapping the other side.
▶ Watch Video
Coaching Cues
›"Hips high in the V"
›"Reach across to the opposite toe"
›"Legs as straight as you can keep them"
›"Full push up every rep"
Muscles
Chest Core Shoulders Hamstrings
Modifications
⬇️
EasierPush up from the knees, tap to the shin.
⬆️
HarderPause in the V before each reach.
🩹
Wrist IssuesUse push-up handles or elevate the hands.

Elevated Bicycle
10 EACH SIDE
▼
Movement
Lie flat on the bench, hands behind the head. Reach the right elbow to the left knee, return, then reach the left elbow to the right knee, return. Repeat.
▶ Watch Video
Coaching Cues
›"Rotate from the ribs, not just the arms"
›"Extend the resting leg long"
›"Slow and controlled — no yanking the neck"
›"Full rotation each side"
Muscles
Obliques Core Hip Flexors Lower Abs
Modifications
⬇️
EasierSlow the pace or keep the resting leg bent.
⬆️
HarderStraighten the resting leg lower to the ground.
🩹
Lower Back IssuesKeep the legs higher and shrink the range.

KB Deadlift to High Pull
6 REPS
▼
Movement
Standing with feet shoulder-width, sit the butt back and grab the KB with both hands. As you stand, pull the elbows back until the bottom of the KB passes the belly button. Return to the start position.
▶ Watch Video
Coaching Cues
›"Stand first, then pull high"
›"Elbows lead the pull, above the hands"
›"Flat back on the way down"
›"Squeeze the upper back at the top"
Muscles
Glutes Upper Back Hamstrings Shoulders
Modifications
⬇️
EasierDeadlift only, skip the high pull.
⬆️
HarderPause at the top of the pull.
🩹
Lower Back IssuesReduce depth and lighten the load.

Modifications

TRX Standing Rollout
10 REPS
▼
Movement
Hold TRX handles in front at chest level with straight arms, slight forward lean. Tighten abs, allow hands to reach forward as deep as comfortable. Pull hands back to chest level.
▶ Watch Video
Coaching Cues
›"Arms stay STRAIGHT the whole time — no bending the elbows"
›"Body is one piece — fall forward as a plank"
›"Only go as far as you can pull yourself back"
›"Pull back with your CORE, not your shoulders"
Muscles
Core Abs Lats Shoulders Glutes Hip Flexors
Modifications
⬇️
EasierWalk feet back — more upright body angle = less load. Smaller reach forward.
⬆️
HarderWalk feet forward — more horizontal = more load. Slower 3-second reach forward.
🩹
Low Back IssuesVery small range. Brace hard before each rep. Stop if you feel any back strain.
