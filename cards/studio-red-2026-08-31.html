DB Step Up to Rev. Lunge
8 EACH SIDE
▼
Movement
Hold the DB at the side. Step back completing a reverse lunge, then step up onto the box with the leg you just stepped back with. Step down with the opposite leg going straight into a reverse lunge. Continue to repeat.
▶ Watch Video
Coaching Cues
›"Lunge, then step up — same leg leads both"
›"Full foot on the box every time"
›"Back knee drops straight down on the lunge"
›"Stand tall to reset between reps"
Muscles
Quads Glutes Hamstrings Calves
Modifications
⬇️
EasierSplit the step-up and lunge into separate moves.
⬆️
HarderAdd a DB in each hand.
🩹
Knee IssuesUse a lower box and pain-free depth.
