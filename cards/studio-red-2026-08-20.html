🏋️ Bench
💪
DB Pullover Hips Off Bench
6 REPS
▼
Movement
Lie on the bench, holding a DB with both hands over the chest. Lower it overhead keeping the ribs down, then pull it back to the starting position. Repeat.
▶ Watch Video
Coaching Cues
›"Ribs down — don't let the back arch"
›"Long arms, soft elbows"
›"Feel the stretch through the lats"
›"Pull back with the lats, not just the arms"

DB Sumo Deadlift 2arm
6 REPS
▼
Movement
Feet wider than the shoulders, toes out. Sit the hips back with a flat back and pick up the DBs by squeezing the glutes to stand tall. Send the hips back to return.
▶ Watch Video
Coaching Cues
›"Knees out over the toes as you sit back"
›"Chest proud — flat back start to finish"
›"Stand by squeezing the glutes, not yanking the back"
›"Bells travel straight up and down between the legs"

Sprinter Abs on Bench
6 EACH SIDE
▼
Movement
Sit on the bench with the upper body at a 45° angle. Bring the right knee toward the chest while pumping the left arm toward the knee, then alternate sides like you're sprinting. Don't let the feet touch the ground or the body fall back.
▶ Watch Video
Coaching Cues
›"Run in place — opposite knee, opposite arm"
›"Stay at 45 — don't sit up tall or fall back"
›"Feet never touch down"
›"Quick arms — they set the pace"
Muscles
Core Hip Flexors Lower Abs Obliques
Modifications
⬇️
EasierTap the heels down between reps, or slow the pace.
⬆️
HarderLean further back or speed up the cadence.
🩹
Lower Back IssuesSit taller and shrink the range.
🔵 Rack
🤸
Slam Ball Roll Over Push Up
6 EACH SIDE
▼
Movement
Start in a high plank with one hand on the med ball, ball under the shoulder. Lower safely as low as you can, push up, then roll the ball to the other hand and repeat.
▶ Watch Video
Coaching Cues
›"Control the wobble — grip the ball"
›"Chest drops level with the hands"
›"Squeeze the glutes — one straight line"
›"Roll smooth — don't rush the handoff"
Muscles
Chest Triceps Shoulders Core
Modifications
⬇️
EasierDrop to the knees, keeping the same depth.
⬆️
HarderSlow the lower or add a pause at the bottom.
🩹
Wrist IssuesUse a wider grip, neutral wrists.
🏐
Ball Split Squat to Single Leg Deadlift
6 EACH SIDE
▼
Movement
Place the med ball behind you and set the right toe on it. Drive the right knee straight down under the hip toward the ground, drive up through the left heel, and at the top, hinge at the hips into a single-leg deadlift.
▶ Watch Video
Coaching Cues
›"Split squat first, then hinge — two distinct phases"
›"Back knee drops straight down under the hip"
›"Flat back on the hinge"
›"Balance through the standing leg"
Muscles
Quads Hamstrings Glutes Core
Modifications
⬇️
EasierSplit the combo into two separate movements.
⬆️
HarderSlow both phases and pause at each bottom.
🩹
Balance IssuesLightly hold a wall or rack for support.
🔁
TRX Get Up
6 REPS
▼
Movement
Start seated on the ground, hands in the straps facing each other. Pull the body up to standing using the arms, fully extending them next to the body at the top. Control yourself back down to tap the bottom and repeat.
▶ Watch Video
Coaching Cues
›"Pull with control — no yanking the straps"
›"Stand tall at the top, arms fully extended"
›"Lower with the same control as you rose"
›"Tap the ground lightly, don't collapse"
Muscles
Quads Core Shoulders Glutes
Modifications
⬇️
EasierUse a bench to sit on instead of the floor.
⬆️
HarderSlow the lower to 3 seconds.
🩹
Knee IssuesUse hands to assist more through the standing phase.
