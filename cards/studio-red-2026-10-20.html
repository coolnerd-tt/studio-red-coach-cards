DB Man Maker
LADDER
▼
Movement
Plank on the DBs. Row each side, lower the chest between the weights, and push back to plank. Jump forward into a squat, stand with a bicep curl, press to the ceiling, lower back down, and jump back to plank.
▶ Watch Video
Coaching Cues
›"One piece at a time — row, row, push up, jump, curl, press"
›"Hips stay quiet on the rows"
›"Stand all the way up before the press"
›"Pace it — smooth beats fast on this one"
Muscles
Shoulders Core Chest Glutes
Modifications
⬇️
EasierStep instead of jump, and skip the push up.
⬆️
HarderHeavier DBs, unbroken reps.
🩹
Wrist IssuesUse hex DBs and keep wrists locked, or row from the knees.

Fitband Anti- Rotation Press
LADDER · EACH SIDE
▼
Movement
Start standing sideways with the hands in the middle of the chest, holding the FitBand handles. Push the hand straight out, keeping the body from rotating, then bring the hands back to the chest and repeat.
▶ Watch Video
Coaching Cues
›"Press out slow — the band wants to spin you"
›"Squeeze the glutes — lock the hips square"
›"The further out the hands, the harder it fights"
›"Breathe out as you press"
Muscles
Core Obliques Shoulders Chest
Modifications
⬇️
EasierStep toward the anchor to reduce the pull.
⬆️
HarderStep away, pause 3 seconds at full extension.
🩹
Shoulder IssuesShorten the press-out range.

DB Fire Hydrant on Bench

▼
Movement

›"Hips stay square — don't roll to lift higher"
›"Hold the DB with the knee, don't let it slip"
›"Slow lower — keep tension the whole set"

LADDER
▼
Movement

›"Straps are for balance, not pulling"
›"Chest tall as you sink"
›"Land soft on the same leg"

KB Shoulder to Shoulder Press
LADDER
▼
Movement
Feet shoulder-width, KB held in front of the right shoulder with elbows pointing down. Press the KB up and over your head, then bend the elbows and slowly lower it to the left shoulder. Keep alternating shoulders.
▶ Watch Video
Coaching Cues
›"Press up and over — draw a rainbow with the bell"
›"Slow lower to the other shoulder — that's the work"
›"Ribs down, glutes tight — no leaning side to side"
›"Catch soft — bell lands gently every time"
Muscles
Shoulders Triceps Core Obliques
Modifications
⬇️
EasierLighten the bell or press straight up without the travel.
⬆️
HarderPause overhead at the top of each arc.
🩹
Shoulder IssuesPress to eye level and shrink the arc.

TRX Ab Tuck to Pike
LADDER
▼
Movement
Feet secured in the TRX handles, holding a high plank with the core engaged. Pull the knees to the chest and extend back to the start, then drive the hips into the air as far as possible and lower back to plank with control. Repeat the combo.
▶ Watch Video
Coaching Cues
›"Tuck, extend, then pike — two moves, one set"
›"Hips high on the pike — legs stay long"
›"Shoulders stacked over the wrists throughout"
›"Control the return — no crashing into plank"
Muscles
Core Hip Flexors Shoulders Lower Abs
Modifications
⬇️
EasierTucks only — add the pike when ready.
⬆️
HarderSlow both moves to a 3-second tempo.
🩹
Shoulder IssuesFloor-based tucks with feet on a slider.
