Bench Wall Sit Press
LADDER 6→1
▼
Movement
Adjust the bench to its highest setting. Sit back with feet hip-width apart, back flat on the bench, and hamstrings parallel to the floor. Complete a shoulder press with the DBs.
▶ Watch Video
Coaching Cues
›"Back flat against the bench the whole set"
›"Hamstrings stay parallel — hold the sit"
›"Press straight up, full lockout"
›"Brace the core through every press"
Muscles
Shoulders Quads Triceps Core
Modifications
⬇️
EasierRaise the wall-sit height or lighten the DBs.
⬆️
HarderLower the sit or slow the press tempo.
🩹
Knee IssuesReduce the wall-sit depth.

DB SLRDL to Curtsy
LADDER 6→1 · EACH SIDE
▼
Movement
Holding a DB in one hand, complete a single-leg RDL with that same leg, then a curtsy lunge with that same leg stepping back. Repeat the combo, then switch the DB for the other side.
▶ Watch Video
Coaching Cues
›"Hinge first, then curtsy — two distinct moves"
›"Flat back on the RDL"
›"Step behind and across on the curtsy"
›"Stand tall between each phase"
Muscles
Glutes Hamstrings Adductors Core
Modifications
⬇️
EasierSplit the combo — all RDLs, then all curtsy lunges.
⬆️
HarderSlow both phases and pause at each bottom.
🩹
Balance IssuesLightly tap the working foot down for stability.
🎗️
Fitband External Rotations
LADDER 6→1 · EACH SIDE
▼
Movement
Connect the Fitband to the top of the inclined bench, standing side-facing the bench with both hands holding the handle, arms fully extended in front of the body. Rotate the torso away from the bench, keeping the arms extended, then return and repeat.
▶ Watch Video
Coaching Cues
›"Rotate from the ribs, arms stay long"
›"Feet stay planted — only the torso turns"
›"Control both directions equally"
›"Keep tension on the band throughout"
Muscles
Obliques Core Shoulders Chest
Modifications
⬇️
EasierStep toward the anchor to reduce tension.
⬆️
HarderStep back for more resistance.
🩹
Lower Back IssuesShrink the rotation range.

🏐
Ball Slam w/ Power Hop
LADDER 6→1
▼
Movement
Start with the med ball between the feet. Pick it up in a squat and hop up as you raise it overhead, then slam it by sending the hips to the back wall, between the big toes.
▶ Watch Video
Coaching Cues
›"Full overhead reach before the slam"
›"Hop with power, land soft"
›"Slam through the ball, hinge as it lands"
›"Reset your stance before the next rep"
Muscles
Shoulders Quads Core Glutes
Modifications
⬇️
EasierRemove the hop — squat and slam only.
⬆️
HarderHeavier ball, continuous rhythm.
🩹
Knee IssuesNo hop — stand tall out of the squat instead.
🔔
KB Deadlift High Pull
LADDER 6→1
▼
Movement
KB held with both hands in front of the thighs, knees soft. Back straight, reach the butt to the rear wall as deep as comfortable, then stand tall and pull the elbows back until the bottom of the KB passes the belly button.
▶ Watch Video
Coaching Cues
›"Stand first, then pull high"
›"Elbows lead the pull, above the hands"
›"Flat back on the way down"
›"Squeeze the upper back at the top"
Muscles
Glutes Upper Back Hamstrings Shoulders
Modifications
⬇️
EasierDeadlift only, skip the high pull.
⬆️
HarderPause at the top of the pull.
🩹
Lower Back IssuesReduce depth and lighten the load.

TRX T Pull
LADDER 6→1
▼
Movement
Start with arms extended, feet under the anchors. Keeping the arms straight, pull them apart to a T position, return, and repeat.
▶ Watch Video
Coaching Cues
›"Straight arms — this is a raise, not a row"
›"T goes wide, squeeze the blades"
›"Plank body — no hip hinge to help"
›"Control the return — full extension every rep"
Muscles
Rear Delts Upper Back Shoulders Core
Modifications
⬇️
EasierWalk the feet back to reduce the angle.
⬆️
HarderWalk the feet forward, pause at the T.
🩹
Shoulder IssuesShorten the range of the pull.
